How a project runs

You get a written quotation before any work starts, and a working machine at the end.

  1. Site survey

    We see the machine, list the inputs and outputs, and agree what it must do.

  2. Quotation

    Itemised hardware and service costs, with model numbers and delivery time.

  3. Build & program

    Panel wiring, PLC and firmware programming, and bench testing before installation.

  4. Commissioning

    Installation, calibration and operator training on site, then support after handover.
